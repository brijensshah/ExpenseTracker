Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address and a password, which we store only in hashed form. As you use Spendly, we also store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to provide and improve Spendly: to sign you in, to record and display your expenses, and to show you summaries and spending patterns. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database on the server that runs Spendly. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can delete your expenses at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any other third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
